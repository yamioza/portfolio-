Pocket Rocket

Design & Development of a Compact Handheld Gaming Device

A compact, modular handheld gaming device designed for retro and indie arcade-style games. Pocket Rocket combines physical tactile button interactions, a vibrant digital display, and custom-designed electronics housed in an ergonomic dual-tone shell.

📌

Design Note from Miro: “The concept was integrating 3D modelling/printing with PCB design with a lil code... taking inspiration from prior projects to create my custom mini play device.”

Industrial Design: Split dual-tone cyan & yellow body, textured grip, rear tabletop kickstand, and lanyard loop.
Controls & Tactile Feel: Responsive D-pad, thumbstick rocker, action switches, and low-latency top shoulder triggers.
Electronics & PCB: Custom handheld PCB layout integrating microcontrollers, display driver, audio amp, and battery management (BMS).
Future Modular Scalability: Expandable bus architecture for swappable game cartridges or wireless link-play.

Designed through 4 iterative 2D aspect ratio studies in Miro, testing display proportions, D-pad ergonomics, and travel depth before moving into parametric 3D CAD modeling.

Schematic and board layout tailored for compact integration. Incorporates battery charge protection, step-up voltage regulation, and clean routed traces for tactile switch matrices.

Low-level C/C++ event loop for debounced microswitch reading, SPI-driven frame buffer rendering, and retro game state machines.

GESTULATOR

The Robotic Hand Gesture Calculator

An interactive computing device inspired by animatronics. The Gestulator calculates arithmetic problems via a physical keypad and articulates computed answers through a 5-finger tendon-driven robotic hand using mechanical number gestures (0 through 9).

💡

Origin Story: “I came up with this being inspired by animatronics. Wanted to make something simple and fun, so I mixed a calculator with an articulated robotic hand!”
